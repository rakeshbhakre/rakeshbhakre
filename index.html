FEATURE 01Hackathon 2026 · Winner

AEGIS

Explainable spacecraft-health monitoring & decision support. Built a spacecraft-health monitoring prototype that turns telemetry (battery voltage, temperature, wheel speed, power) into explainable fault alerts and safety-checked response recommendations through a detect → explain → recommend → safety-check workflow.

Added telemetry-quality checks for missing, stale, and noisy data so sensor or data issues are not mistaken for hardware faults; combined statistical and ML detection over multi-channel patterns to rank likely root causes with matched and contradicting evidence; and separated detection from action approval using a rule-based guard over a predefined action library. In a saved simulation benchmark with fault injection, detected 29 of 40 injected fault episodes (72.5%), including 5 of 5 reaction-wheel friction episodes.
